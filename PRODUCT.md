# ARMIS product

## Register

product

## Users

ARMIS owners inspect their persistent Mac mini agent system and its companies through an interactive city.

## Product Purpose

Reflect declared agents, their authority, work and rest in a precise island city. Selection reveals jobs and evidence. Demo activity remains explicitly simulated until live integration is connected.

## Brand Personality

Precise, distinctive, purposeful. Buildings and rooms communicate the work they house.

## Anti-references

Floating buildings, overlapping signs, actors passing through furniture, excessive labels and decorative activity presented as execution.

## Design Principles

- Geometry and system meaning must agree.
- Preserve real identities and responsibility structure.
- Give each company character through equipment and materials.
- Keep operational cues restrained and inspectable.
- Missing observation remains unknown.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve keyboard selection, sharp DOM text, hover names and reduced motion. State cues use shape and text as well as color.
